## Editorial controls

- Approved external references: Allianz, Malaysian or relevant foreign public authorities/regulators, FMOS, and reputable news sources.
- Do not cite competing insurers as authorities.
- Use `www.casb2u.com` for canonical, Open Graph, schema and hreflang URLs.
- Every English article requires the same Chinese slug, and vice versa.
- Every indexed article requires a unique feature image, except deliberate language reuse of the same image.
- Prefer a focused 5–8 minute article. Longer reference guides require a clear reason and an executive summary.
